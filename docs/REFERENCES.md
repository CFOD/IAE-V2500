# Reference sources

What the model is built from, best first. Public sources are linked. Training material the project owner
supplied is listed by title only: it stays out of the repository.

## Public, in-service photos and drawings

- **NTSB docket ENG14IA028:** JetBlue A320 N656JB, V2500-A5, uncontained HPT failure and fire under the
  cowls, Long Beach, 2014. The *Powerplants Group Chairman's Factual Report* (49 photos, 2 figures) is the
  best public source for the core externals. It has photos of:
  - the washed engine, right and left sides;
  - the three compressor bleed valves with their seals;
  - the ECS duct, the pressure regulating valve and its Marman clamps;
  - the VSV actuator, the stage 2.5 bleed actuators and their fuel lines;
  - the white, red-striped harnesses with AS622-series ID sleeves and red-brown cushion clamps;
  - the lower bifurcation fire seal with its laced harnesses;
  - the turbine case cooling valve actuator;
  - a generic V2500 cross-section with the flange letters.

  Docket: https://data.ntsb.gov/Docket/?NTSBNumber=ENG14IA028
- **NTSB docket ENG14IA001:** Spirit A319, V2524-A5, engine failure, Dallas-Fort Worth, 2013. The
  *Powerplants Group Chairman's Factual Report* (35 pages, 38 photos) has two side-on shop photos of the
  engine on its stand, plus the EGT harness junction box, the No. 5 bearing scavenge line and the V2500
  flange diagram. Docket: https://data.ntsb.gov/Docket/?NTSBNumber=ENG14IA001
- **Wikimedia Commons, category IAE V2500:** a display engine, IAE's cutaway model, MTU Museum turbine
  cases, a reverser deployed in flight, on-wing views.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:IAE_V2500
- **Patent US 6,764,045** (Boeing), figures 1 and 2: the conventional cascade reverser's blocker door and
  drag link geometry.

## Supplied by the project owner (not in the repository)

- Airbus A319/A320/A321 technical training manual, *70 Power Plant (V2500-A5)*. Used for component
  location, the reverser design, the fluid disconnect panel, the fan electrical connector panel, the core
  electrical junction box, the air system, the oil system and cowl opening.
- SR Technics *V2500 Component Location Manual* (issue 2.04, 2010): right-hand and left-hand fan case and
  core pages.
- MTU Maintenance Hannover, *V2500 A1 & A5/D5 Familiarization*: modules, bearings, gearbox, drains and the
  bifurcation panel.
- IAE service bulletin V2500-ENG-72-0660 (No. 5 bearing oil tubes). It cites the publication numbers below.
- In-service and shop photos: engines on transport stands, on slings, with the cowls open.

## Not available publicly

These are distributed under licence through the Pratt & Whitney/IAE and Airbus customer portals:
- **V2500-A5 Series Illustrated Parts Catalog (EIPC):** P&W part number 2A4428. IAE IETM refs for the
  V2527/V2527E are S-V2500-7IA/7IB (AQ02/AQ03) and 7SA/7SB/7NA/7NB (SQ02-SQ05). Chapters 73-80 hold the
  external tubes, harnesses, brackets and clamps.
- **V2500 A1/A5 Series Engine Manual:** P&W part number 2A4407 (E-V2500-1IA).
- **Airbus A318/A319/A320/A321 Aircraft Illustrated Parts Catalog (AIPC):** chapters 54 (pylon), 71 (power
  plant) and 78 (thrust reverser) hold the nacelle and the parts Airbus adds to make the engine installable.
